# Lumen Launcher — Privacy Policy

[Español](es.md)

_Effective date: October 17, 2026_

Lumen is a home screen (launcher) for Android. It is built to keep your data on your phone: Lumen has no servers and no user accounts, shows no ads, and includes no analytics or tracking tools. The developer never receives your personal data.

This policy explains what Lumen accesses, why, and where that information goes.

## Summary

| What | Why | Leaves your phone? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| List of installed apps | To show and open your apps | No |
| Approximate location (optional) | Weather on the home screen | Rounded coordinates go to MET Norway |
| Media playing in other apps (optional) | The player under the clock | No |
| App usage statistics (optional) | Your screen-time screen | No |
| Photos you pick (optional) | Using your photo as wallpaper | No |
| Emergency contact (optional) | Shown on your lock screen | No |
| Settings and app customizations | Remembering your choices | Only in your own Android backup |

## In detail

**Installed apps.** Lumen reads the list of apps that have a launcher icon, so it can show, search and open them. Names you give apps, favorites, and hidden or locked apps are stored only on your phone.

**Approximate location and weather (optional, off by default).** If you turn on weather and allow approximate location, Lumen gets your approximate position from Android. It rounds the coordinates to two decimals (about 1 km) and sends them to the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway, api.met.no) to get the forecast. No other information about you is sent. On your phone, Lumen keeps only the latest forecast and the rounded coordinates of that request, so it doesn't ask again for the same place too soon. MET Norway's handling of requests is covered by its own terms and privacy policy: <https://api.met.no/doc/TermsOfService>. You can turn weather off or revoke the location permission at any time.

**Notification access (optional).** If you grant notification access, Lumen uses it only to see which app is playing music or other media, so it can show the track and its controls under the clock. Lumen does not read, store or send the content of your notifications.

**Usage access (optional).** If you grant usage access, Lumen reads Android's usage events (time spent in apps, app opens and unlocks) when you open its statistics screen. These figures are calculated on your phone, shown to you, and not stored or sent anywhere.

**Photos (optional).** When you choose one of your photos as a wallpaper, you pick it with Android's photo picker, so Lumen sees only the photo you chose. Lumen keeps a resized copy in its private storage, so it can redraw the wallpaper later. It deletes copies that are no longer used.

**Emergency contact (optional).** The name, phone number and note you enter are stored on your phone and drawn on your lock screen wallpaper, so that whoever finds your phone can contact you. Anyone who looks at your lock screen can see them, so only enter what you are comfortable showing.

**App lock.** Locked apps ask for your fingerprint, face or screen lock through Android's own prompt. Lumen never sees or stores your biometric data or your PIN; Android only tells it whether you were verified.

**Wallpapers.** When you apply a wallpaper, Lumen sets it on your home and lock screen through Android.

**Backup.** If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may back up Lumen's settings and customizations (and, when you move to a new phone, the photos used as wallpapers). That backup is managed by Google under your account, not by the developer. You control it in your phone's settings.

**Purchases.** If you buy Lumen Pro, the payment is processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive your payment details. Lumen only checks with Google Play whether the purchase is valid.

## Sharing and selling

The developer does not collect your data, so there is nothing to sell or share. The only information that leaves your phone is the rounded location sent to MET Norway when weather is on.

## Children

Lumen is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Your choices

All data-related features are optional. You can turn each one off in Lumen's settings, revoke permissions in Android's settings, or uninstall Lumen. Uninstalling removes all data Lumen stored on your phone.

## Changes

If Lumen starts handling data differently, for example by adding a feature that needs new access, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
